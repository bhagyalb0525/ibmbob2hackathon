### Patch Explanation

**Patch summary**: Replace `Math.trunc` with `Math.round` on line 37 of `demo_service/src/services/cart.ts`.

**Why**: `convertVoucherDiscount()` uses `Math.trunc()` to convert the voucher discount amount from a foreign currency to the cart currency. For exchange-rate multiplications that produce a fractional result (e.g. 10 EUR × 1.08 = 10.8 USD), `Math.trunc` silently discards the fraction, returning 10 instead of 11. This under-applies the discount by 1 USD, inflating the cart grandTotal and triggering the downstream PAYMENT_MISMATCH_ERROR / 500 response.
